# Pass 2 — Grand Roof Reconstruction

This pass replaces arbitrary interior grid roof sampling with footprint-aware nested contours and adds shallow dimensional Gothic dormer assemblies (window faces, stone jambs, sills and mullions). Authored roof type/rise and original `.haunt` stay unchanged.

**Scope:** compiled roof GLB geometry for all students, not a manually decorated Ferre-specific roof.

**Not yet certified:** dormers do not cut through roof surfaces; exact valleys, watertight joints, and browser appearance still need visual QA. A steep Gothic roof must not silently replace a flat roof authored by a student.
