# Gothic Craftsmanship — architectural pass

Adds exterior-only layered stone lancet surrounds, window tracery, projecting stone piers with bases and caps, carved frieze dentils, and deeper façade relief. Uses each floor’s actual room-union footprint and authored opening coordinates. These are decorative static meshes; original `.haunt` files are not mutated, and the ornamental heads do not alter structural aperture heights or gameplay collision.

QA: Compiled source meshes, verified named Gothic nodes and source SHA-256, archive integrity. Browser-rendered visual quality and gameplay collision remain unverified.
