Blocking release conditions

No staircase can receive Gameplay PASS until a player-sized collision sweep confirms the lower corridor → landing → treads → upper landing → upper corridor route, including roof/ceiling headroom, handrails, door swing, and unsupported edges. Stair mesh bounding-box checks are not a substitute.
