Adopt proven game-engine ideas

  1. Separate render and collision geometry.
  2. Use a kinematic capsule with autostep, skin width and ground snapping.
  3. Generate incline proxies for flights and curved segmented proxies for spirals.
  4. Preserve shaft walls and overhead collision.
  5. Certify with a real continuous movement trace, up and down.
